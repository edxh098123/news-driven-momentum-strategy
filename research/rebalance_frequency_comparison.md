# Frequency comparison specification

The 10 existing monthly-long-only signal definitions and fixed Kalman parameters are reused without modification. The same completed-month signal window and historical membership/tradability filters are evaluated at each scheduled formation date; weekly ranking can change due to membership or tradability even when the completed-month inputs are unchanged. Orders execute at the following trading close. Main schedules are final trading sessions of each week, month, calendar quarter, or calendar year. Positions drift naturally between executions, including distributions settling in cash; costs and fully funded targets use the existing engine. Main paths begin with a signal formed on 2020-12-31 and execute at the first 2021 close.

Annual phase 1, 4, 7 and 10 schedules form at the preceding December, March, June and September month-end and execute in the indicated month. All phases start from the same portfolio signal formed on 2020-12-31 and executed at the first 2021 close; thereafter the annual rebalance follows the assigned phase. This common initial bridge avoids missing pre-2021 factor history and ensures comparable initial holdings. January is the main annual path; the first holding period in other phases is shorter than a year. All phase results are retained.

Risk-matched experiments for the three specified signals target the S&P 500's trailing 63-session volatility at each formation using the trailing 63 daily total returns of the newly selected, equal-weight candidate basket. Require at least 42 paired observations, use only data available by formation close, and set exposure to min(1, benchmark volatility / candidate-basket volatility). If insufficient data, hold cash. Exposure is fixed until the next scheduled rebalance subject to natural drift. No leverage or borrowing; uninvested cash earns zero (not the risk-free rate). This is an ex-ante volatility-target approximation, not guaranteed realized equal volatility; costs are applied to actual trades. The comparison to the index is sample-specific, not independent validation.

Daily stock P&L is reported as contribution to initial 2021 portfolio NAV, before trading costs, with PERMNO and an as-of-year-end-or-latest-earlier CRSP name. Annual/stock contributions are dollar contributions to initial NAV, not individually compounded holding returns. Holding durations count consecutive observed trading sessions and are left-censored at 2021 start and right-censored at 2025 end; continuous holding across rebalances is one run. Daily HHI=sum(squared NAV weights), top-three weight, maximum weight and cash are shown. Monthly contributions are linked within each calendar year to the year's starting NAV, and the top-three-positive-month share is measured against the sum of positive monthly contributions, not total net return. For incomplete paths, attribution is not presented as a complete result.

Long-only accounting, CRSP V2 missing-return rules, historical membership, price/return data and the 2020 initialization match the preceding monthly experiment. The monthly regression output compares returns, costs, Sharpe, drawdown and turnover with the prior valid run. FF factors are current historical versions; publication vintages and revisions cannot be fully verified, so strict point-in-time vintage availability remains a limitation of residual signals. All rankings are exploratory in-sample.
