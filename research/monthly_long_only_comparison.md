# Monthly long-only momentum experiment

Formation is the last NYSE trading day each month, execution is the following trading day's close, and the unchanged CRSP engine accounts for distributions, delistings and missing observations. Long targets are ten equal 10% post-transaction-cost weights, funded without borrowing. Fewer than ten eligible securities results in cash (subject to the engine's existing untradeable-holding rule). The final 2020 trading day forms the initial signal; capital is invested at the close of the first trading session in 2021, with its initial transaction cost recorded that day. Costs are 10 bps per dollar traded; borrow cost is zero.

The 12–1 naming convention is ambiguous relative to the project's existing 12–2 implementation: the latter already skips the latest complete month. To satisfy both explicit requirements, 12–1 compounds **12 completed monthly returns** after month-end mark −14 through mark −2, ending one complete month before formation; existing 12–2 is unchanged and compounds the **11 monthly returns** after mark −13 through mark −2. Both skip the latest complete month; they differ by the oldest included month. Intermediate uses mark −13 through −7. Kalman versions use the same respective boundaries and sum causal filtered log returns, then expm1.

Residual and continuous use existing methods unchanged: FF3 standardized one-step residual momentum and PRET-quintile then information-discreteness double sort, respectively. Kalman residual is an experimental extension: first filter stock monthly log returns, transform each filtered value with expm1, and apply the existing 36-month rolling FF3 one-step residual calculation and 11-month standardized residual ranking. It is not the original paper's signal. Kalman continuous is an experimental extension: first filter stock monthly log returns; calculate PRET from their sum, calculate the information-discreteness statistic from signs of the **11 filtered monthly trends** (rather than original daily observations), sort PRET into quintiles and select the ten lowest-ID names in the winner quintile. No mixed score weights are introduced. The monthly observation scale follows the existing Kalman model and makes this a different signal from the paper's daily-sign version.

Kalman settings are unchanged: phi=0.95, Q/R=0.05, initialize with the first 24 consecutive observed monthly returns and then recurse forward; missing observations receive prediction only, never a fabricated signal observation. Input starts in 2015-01 so that filtered residuals can initialize 24 months, estimate 36 months of prior FF3 exposures, and accumulate 11 lagged residuals before 2021 where history is available. Initialization may start later for newer securities, using only available past data. Current WRDS factor histories can be revised and lack point-in-time release vintages; the existing factor signal lags the most recent month, but precise historical vintage availability cannot be verified. The ranking is exploratory in-sample evidence, not out-of-sample proof. Annual alpha is daily CAPM excess-return intercept annualized by 252; annual excess return is strategy net compounded return minus S&P total return.
