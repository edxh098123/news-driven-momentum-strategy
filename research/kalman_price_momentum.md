# Kalman price momentum: fixed experiment specification

The existing Baseline and Intermediate signals, engine, historical membership, timing and costs are unchanged. Month-end marks are those of the project's exchange calendar. Baseline compounds daily total returns after completed mark −13 through mark −2 (11 completed months); Intermediate uses mark −13 through mark −7 (six months). Monthly log total returns are formed by compounding daily CRSP total returns within each complete trading month and applying log1p. A month with any missing daily return, or a total return <= −100%, is unavailable for signal formation.

For each PERMNO, starting with observations from 2017-01-01, wait for the first 24 consecutive valid months. Their mean initializes the state; their sample variance initializes both R and the state variance P, with R floored at 1e-12; Q=0.05R and phi=0.95. R is then fixed, and recursion starts the following month. Consequently these calibration months precede each emitted filtered value, including for securities that enter later. This finite query history is the complete available history for this experiment, not all historical CRSP observations. Securities with insufficient initialization history are ineligible for a filtered ranking.

Each month predicts state phi*mu and variance phi^2*P+Q, then on an observed log return applies K=Ppred/(Ppred+R), mu=pred+K*(observation-pred), P=(1-K)*Ppred. Missing months advance prediction without emitting a return; no backward smoother or future-dependent estimation is used. For the two formation windows, sum emitted monthly trends and apply expm1; require observed and filtered values for every month in the chosen window. The parameters are research assumptions, not fitted to the 2021–2025 evaluation period.

Ranking stability compares successive weekly formations on securities eligible and having both raw and filtered scores at both dates, using rank Spearman correlation, absolute percentile-rank changes, raw log-score changes, and top/bottom-10 retention. Both all-week and calendar-month-boundary summaries are shown. A smoother score or greater retention does not by itself establish predictive improvement. Historical missing observations can be P1 cumulative CRSP returns: a month containing an actual missing daily return is excluded from that formation window, and no inferred daily prices are used. Portfolio returns and unresolved delistings use the existing engine and missing-return handler. Strict incomplete and hypothetical terminal-loss scenarios remain separate.
